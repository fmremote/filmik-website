SCENE CARDS: MODULE DETAIL
--------------------------
Eyebrow
Scene Cards

Headline
Turn scripts into collaborative action plans.

Description
Filmik redefines how departments break down and prepare scripts by transforming
static text into actionable, collaborative data sheets.

Automated Script Breakdown
Automatically identify relevant action information and use it to pre-populate
Scene Cards, giving the department an organized starting point to review and
expand.

Shared Scene Information
Keep characters, action notes, equipment, and safety details together in one
card that the appropriate team members can access.

Ready for Department Review
Refine stunt sequences, equipment, performer details, and notes together as
the scene develops, without scattering the work across documents.

Standardized Scene Numbering
Use combined season-episode-scene numbering for block shoots, avoiding
duplicate scene numbers across scripts.


CALENDARS & SCHEDULING: MODULE DETAIL
-------------------------------------
Headline
Keep large teams in sync, wherever they work.

Description
Schedule your team, organize production dates, and keep everyone aligned as
the schedule changes across departments.

Multi-View Team Board
Filter crews by daily schedules, weekly calendars, departments, roles, or
episodes to make large team coordination easier.

Reuse Schedule Details
Move shoots, fittings, and rehearsals to new dates without rebuilding the
details your team already needs.

Smart Time Zone Architecture
Location shifts automatically adjust notification timing and display clear zone
conversions so nobody misses a call time.

Week Lock Protection
Protect locked performer schedules with explicit warnings and admin overrides
to help prevent costly cancellation penalties.
